What we export

Value-added means the product leaves India cleaned, processed and packed, not as raw commodity. Tell us what your market needs and we will source and process to match.

Spices & masalas

Whole and ground spices, custom masala blends, in retail packs or bulk.

Pickles & chutneys

Mango, lime, mixed vegetable and regional styles, in jars or pouches.

Fruit pulps & purees

Mango, guava, papaya and more, processed for beverage and dessert makers.

Dehydrated vegetables

Onion, garlic, ginger, tomato and mixed flakes or powders for food manufacturers.

Millet & grain products

Millet flours, flakes, snacks and ready mixes for health-focused shelves.

Nuts, jaggery & sweeteners

Roasted cashew, jaggery powder and blocks, and traditional sweet ingredients.
